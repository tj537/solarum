# Architecture

Solarum is one Rust binary: an [egui](https://github.com/emilk/egui) window (eframe 0.31) and a synthesizer engine that runs inside the audio callback ([cpal](https://github.com/RustAudio/cpal)). Nothing is loaded from disk except the saved state and your own sounds (`library.rs`) – fonts and the icon are compiled in, every built-in sound is computed. The only network access is the optional update check at start (`update.rs`).

## Threads and data flow

```
 UI thread (egui)                         audio thread (cpal callback)
 ───────────────                          ────────────────────────────
 SolarumApp.song ── Cmd::Song(Box<Song>) ──▶ Engine.song
 pads / keys    ── Cmd::Hit, Fx, Drop, … ──▶ Engine
 patterns, song ── Cmd::Queue, Arrange, Jump ──▶ Engine
               ◀── Event::Song(song, Change) ── auto mode, recording, song mode
               ◀── Shared (atomics): step, hits per track, level, build-up, spectrum bands
 MIDI callback  ── Cmd (pads, transpose, filter, echo) ──▶ Engine   (no UI round trip)
                ── Midi ──▶ UI (macros, display)
```

- `Song` is `Copy` (fixed arrays), so it travels whole through the channel. The UI sends it whenever it changed this frame (`song != sent`).
- The engine owns its copy. When it changes the song itself – auto mode, a recorded hit, the drop – it sends it back with a `Change` the UI shows as a caption.
- The UI reads the playhead and hit counters from `Shared` atomics; the smooth beam interpolates between steps with the frame time.

## Engine

`Engine::render` fills an interleaved stereo buffer sample by sample:

1. **Clock** – `phase` counts 16th steps; a step fires when the integer part changes (odd steps wait for the swing offset). Bar starts call `new_bar`: drops, a pattern waiting from `Cmd::Queue` (it comes in now; with the auto mode on a `Fill::Roll` led into it, by hand it just switches), song mode (`arrange_bar`), and in auto mode the arrangement. It runs in cycles (`cycle_start`, `cycle_len`: 32–64 bars); every drop – also one by hand – starts the next cycle (`new_cycle`), so an automatic drop never follows a manual one closely. Everything in a cycle counts from its start, so changes and fills land on 8-bar phrases. **The set's arc:** every cycle has a `heat` (0…1, the first 0.5) that climbs 0.15–0.35 per cycle to a peak (≥ 0.9) and then falls back to 0.3–0.5 – the set breathes out and climbs again, a peak every three or four cycles. The heat picks the `Plan` (`plans_for`: calm cycles get short-filtered, filtered or the roller, middle ones also the classic, the peak classic or stripped; never the same plan twice in a row) and the length (the peak 32 bars, calm cycles 48–64 in the calm vibes), and sets how high `curve_target` goes. Plans: classic, short and filtered, stripped, filtered and roller (no breakdown: the groove rolls into the build-up); each has its own breakdown `depth`, so the layers take out different things (the short one keeps the bass, stripped leaves hats and synth). A cycle: groove with a change every `auto_bars` (1–16) picked by `ear::choose` (see Generator) – the big change on a phrase start, small touches in between (`gen::Recent` keeps the auto mode off the tracks it changed last) – and a one-bar `Fill` closing a phrase (a big one – `BIG_FILLS` – every 16 bars, a light one in between, never the same twice in a row, `Fill::Throw` into a breakdown), the breakdown (the plan's tracks muted, optionally under a low-pass that opens by itself), 2 bars build-up, drop. A new chapter (`gen::new_chapter`) comes after at least two drops – right after the peak (then `bold`: always two changes), otherwise by a 35 % chance, at the latest after four: one or two of a new bassline, a new synth line, another sound, other chords (refitted notes) or, rarely, another key. Breakdowns, build-ups and drops only come with AUTO FX (`Song::auto_fx`) on; without it the auto mode just evolves the groove (no mutes) and fills. A breakdown restores the mutes it saved when it ends, also when the music stops, the auto mode is switched off or a song starts. With AUTO FX on, the auto mode never leaves a track out by its layer: `energy_mutes` mutes a track when the energy crosses below its layer (`energy_out` remembers which) and unmutes it when the energy comes back – only crossings count, so a track you unmute stays on; the playback gate then ignores `LAYERS`. AUTO FX also moves the mix (`drift_knobs`): the `KNOBS` (eight track volumes, dark, space, low/mid/high EQ) glide towards goals within `KNOB_RANGE` of their home, a couple of new goals every two bars (`new_knob_goals`), back home when the auto mode or AUTO FX is off. A `Cmd::Song` whose knob differs from what the engine last sent (`knob_sent`) is a hand on it: that value becomes its home. Every build-up (DROP, drop zone, auto mode, song mode) starts on a bar and lasts two bars and is made from the groove itself (`build_play`): the kick rolls (quarters, 8ths, 16ths – also when energy or a breakdown took it out, not when it's muted by hand), the hats run in 16ths, the open hat rests, bass, synth, rim and ride loop the second bar's first beat, then half a beat, the synths an octave up for the last quarter. The last `DROP_GAP` steps are silent, then `drop_now` lands on the bar after. On top, every build-up has a gate that chops harder with the progress and a light high-pass that lets go within a few milliseconds after the drop (`build_cut`). Outside build-ups Solarum never touches the effects.
2. **Steps** – every track is one bar of 16 steps. `Track::len` stays in the file format, but `sanitize` writes a shorter loop from older saves out as its repeats (`Track::expand`), and the generator does the same (a half-bar loop is the first half copied into the second), so there is no loop length in the UI. `chance` < 100 rolls the dice. A closed hat never plays on a step where the open hat plays. Energy (`layer_energy`, taken on every beat) gates the percussion live: below 45 it drops unaccented hits, above 60 it adds ghost hats (chosen by a hash of track, step and 2-bar block, so it forms a pattern). Synth steps whose next step slides get a longer gate and glide.
3. **Voices** – `trigger` applies the track's `tune` and `decay` and, for bass and synth, the chord progression (`Song::prog_degree` of the bar, as scale degrees), then picks the model from `Track::sound` (`KICKS`, `HATS`, `OPEN_HATS`, `RIDES` tables in `synth.rs`; clap, rim, bass and synth switch on a `model` index). The synth's stab plays the scale's triad on the step's note.
4. **Mixer** (pan, delay send, reverb send per track; stabs get extra echo; pump ducks everything but the kick and the returns with an envelope the kick restarts) → rumble (kick through dark resonant delays, ducked by the kick) → drop boom / crash → build-up riser (fades out in the gap before the drop).
5. **Master** – wash (everything above ~280 Hz into a huge reverb, dry ducked; the room shrinks slowly after it, so the tail rings out), spring-back filter (or the breakdown's auto filter), build-up high-pass (lets go within a few ms after the drop instead of jumping), darkness tone filter (18 kHz down to ~1.5 kHz), master EQ (SVF low shelf / bell / high shelf, ±5 dB – `song::EQ_DB`), crush (one fixed lo-fi sound faded in over ~30 ms: sample-and-hold at `CRUSH_RATE`, companded steps, lightly rounded off), gate (16th chop), saturation, a simple limiter, master volume, then tape stop (a 2-second ring buffer read back at a falling rate; let go and it spins back up, landing on the live output, then crossfades to it). Last, eight band-passes measure the spectrum for the visualizer (`Shared::bands`).

**Energy** is the arrangement, never the volume: a track is muted when `layer_energy` falls below `LAYERS[track]` and unmuted when it comes back (`energy_mutes`) (hats and synth always, then rim, clap, kick at 0.3, bass, open hat, ride at 0.7 – so at 85 everything plays), checked on every beat so tracks come and go in time. Below `BREAK_ZONE` (0.3) it's a break: kick and bass out, a low-pass closes (`break_lp`) and the reverb sends grow, following `energy_smooth`. Track volumes, kick drive and the master saturation no longer depend on it. A drop lifts the energy to at least 0.85. By hand there is no drop zone any more – the energy starts full (1.0, also after a clear) and only DROP drops; `DROP_ZONE` is left for the song's slim bar. When Solarum steers (`steers_energy`: the auto mode with `Song::auto_fx`, or a song), the engine owns the energy: `curve_target` (or `Part::energy` in song mode) sets `energy_goal` every bar (lands high on the drop and settles over 8 bars, then one level per phrase up to the top in the last phrase before the breakdown, the plan's depth in it creeping up towards the build-up, climbing through the build-up; the cycle's heat sets drop, settle and top) and the energy glides there by at most `GLIDE` per step; a `Cmd::Song` from the UI can't change it. The UI then shows the energy read-only: in the log for the auto mode, as a slim bar ("SONG") for a song; `Song::curve` is no longer used. With AUTO FX off the auto mode only calls `gen::evolve(…, mutes: false)` – patterns, sounds and notes, never a track in or out – and switching AUTO FX off brings every unlocked track back (the UI) and lets a running breakdown go (the engine).

**Edits vs. engine songs**: every `Event::Song` carries how many `Cmd::Song` the engine had taken in (`songs_in`); the UI ignores a song older than its own last edit (`songs_sent`), so the engine's frequent songs (the energy glide sends one every beat) can't undo a click.

**Song mode** (`Arrangement`: the patterns, a form from `form::FORMS`, and a fallback song for when no pattern is saved): `arrange_bar` moves to the next section when one is over or on a `Cmd::Jump`, and `enter_section` loads the section's pattern (`form::pattern_slot`: an empty slot falls back to the nearest full one before it), sets the part's energy (`Part::energy`, every bar after; the last two bars before a break ease off to 0.6), mutes kick and bass for a `Break` (a `Breakdown`, so a pattern change keeps them out) and drops into every `Peak`. Grooves and peaks get a gentle change every 8 bars (`gen::vary`: new notes, a new hat or percussion pattern or sound – no mutes, no shifted rhythms) and the acid sweep runs as in the auto mode; a peak that keeps the same favorite starts a `gen::new_chapter` of it. The intro climbs from 0.05 (hats under the break filter) and the outro sinks to 0, so tracks come and go one by one. A break builds up over its last two bars; a fill leads into every break and into a section with another favorite, plus random fills every 8 bars in grooves and peaks. The forms (`form::FORMS`) drop two or three times, the first after 20–28 bars: favorite 1, then 2 after the first drop, then 1 again for the last. After the last section the engine stops and sends `Change::SongEnd`. The section and the bar it started go to `Shared::part` / `part_start` for the strip.

`render_offline` runs the same engine without a device – for tests and the demo loops.

**Recording** – a song records itself from its first section; `Cmd::Capture(true)` records the whole session instead (`capturing`: across songs until `Cmd::Capture(false)`, but only while the music plays – armed while stopped, a pause is left out). `keep_recording` appends every rendered buffer and sends `Event::Audio` pieces of `REC_CHUNK` samples; when it ends, `end_recording` sets `rec_tail` to two seconds, and once that is recorded `finish_recording` sends the rest and `Event::Recorded`. The UI keeps the pieces as 16-bit (`wav::to_i16`), offers *Save WAV* and writes it on a thread (`wav::write_i16`).

Macros act live (energy: the layers, percussion density and ghost hats (only while SOLAR OS plays – auto or song mode – and `Shared::touches`, the setting, allows it: `Engine::touches`; by hand the pattern plays exactly as written), hat length, bass brightness and the break zone; dark: kick length, rumble, tone; acid: filter and resonance – the auto mode sweeps it slowly; space: sends and feedback) and the generator uses Energy for density.

The filter pad sends `Cmd::Filter` (position) and `Cmd::FilterRes` (resonance: Q 1.6 at rest up to about 16, smoothed in the engine). Live effects are `Cmd::Fx(Fx, bool)` – held down / let go. The UI sends a change only when key or mouse state changes (`fx_sent`).

## App state around the song

- **Undo/redo** – at the end of every frame the UI compares `song` with `sent` (the last song it gave the engine). A difference is a user edit: the previous song goes on the undo stack, unless the last edit was less than 0.8 s ago (a slider move or a painted row is one step). Songs coming from the engine don't count; neither does the restore itself (`restoring`). Undo and slot loads keep the current master volume and auto-mode settings (`keep_live_settings`).
- **Locks** – `Track::lock`: shuffle uses `gen::shuffle` (three `gen::generate_keeping` grooves with the locked tracks, the one with the largest `gen::distance` to the groove before wins – key, mood, tempo, sounds, mutes and moved steps – so two shuffles never sound alike; each groove also gets its own drive, ±0.2 around the energy, for busier or sparser patterns), the dice is disabled, `gen::evolve` and `gen::new_chapter` skip them.
- **Files** – *File* menu: `.solarum` save/open (also by drag and drop). A loaded file is an ordinary edit, so undo goes back.
- **Window** – eframe restores the last window state, so a quit inside a visualizer would come back fullscreen; `new` always leaves fullscreen.
- **Visualizers** – LIGHTS: `visual: bool`; the window then shows only `visual::visual` in fullscreen, keys keep working, Esc or a double-click leaves. FACE (`face_window`) opens an immediate viewport of its own (`face_viewport`), so the main window stays usable; double-click or F toggles its fullscreen, closing it ends it. Without devices (tests) FACE still fills the main window (`visual` + `face`). The face notices the drop itself (the build-up ending) and keeps that moment in egui temp data.
- **Tracks** – the energy mutes what it takes out (see below). How often the auto mode changes something sits under SET in SOLAR OS (a `widgets::stops` slider, 1–16 bars) together with the AUTO FX switch – now behind the computer's gear. With AUTO FX on, the energy leaves the right panel and shows as a level meter in the log (`Log::ui`'s `energy`).
- **The computer** – the auto mode is the terminal under the mood (`computer`): off (no auto mode, no song) its screen is dark with a power button; on it boots (a line of light opening, `power_at`), Solari (`log::robot`) waves and types one of `log::HELLOS` for three seconds, and then it shows the log. On, it's SOLAR OS (`os_chrome`, `os_prompt`): a title bar with an LED, SET (`auto_settings`, in the same SOLAR OS green as VIBE: change every 1–16 bars – 4 by default – and AUTO FX) and OFF (the auto mode off, or the song stopped), a menu with PICK A GENRE (`vibe_list`: "What should Solari play?", every vibe with its line, and Surprise me) and SHUFFLE (`dice_all`: `roll_track` on every track that's on and unlocked; also the `shuffle` command), the log (`Log::ui` keeps room above and below), and Solari's prompt at the bottom (`command`). There is no AUTO MODE button in the right panel any more.
- **Start and Solari's console** – `new` turns the loaded song into an empty sky (`custom`; the first undo brings the last groove back) with the sound section open. `toggle_auto` folds the sound away when the auto mode comes on (and opens it when it goes off) Switching it on (`power`) starts the music and `start_growing` sends `Cmd::Grow` with `gen::grow_target` – the song as it is plus a fitting part for every empty track; the engine brings one of them in per bar (kick, hat, clap, bass, rim, open hat, synth, ride) before the auto mode goes on as usual, so a beat built by hand is kept and an empty sky builds up. The auto mode is always off at the start. The commands (`command`: shuffle, seed, vibe, vibes, drop, fx, every, mood, bpm, status, joke, help, off; `seed <word>` hashes the word (`seed_of`, FNV-1a) into an `Rng` for `gen::generate_keeping` – unlike shuffle it ignores the groove before, so a seed and a vibe always roll the same beat) get Solari's answers through `bunk`; the top bar has no vibe picker any more. While it has the keyboard (`typing`), `keys` leaves it alone. The settings float over the space window (`space_rect`).
- **Metronome and clear** – the tempo menu's metronome sends `Cmd::Metronome` (the engine clicks on every beat, higher on the one; `click`, `click_one`), the broom next to undo clears (`clear`: `Song::empty` with the tempo, volume, auto settings and locked tracks kept – sound, EQ, pitch, volumes and the mood back at the start). Ghost hats only play on a hat track that has steps, so an empty pattern is silent.
- **Energy mutes everywhere** – what the energy takes out is muted (`energy_mutes`), by hand, in the auto mode and in songs, and also while stopped (a `Cmd::Song` runs it then); the planets view's sun shines in `widgets::energy_color` (red through a build-up); there is no OUT tag and no layer gate in the playback any more.
- **Log** – `say` writes into `log::Log` (no captions in the space window any more). Every frame `log_state` reads what plays and `Log::tick` adds the countdowns on new bars (a new cycle with its heat, breakdown in 8 and 4, drop in 16 and 8) and every 30–50 s while playing a fact about the set (minutes, drops – `note_drop`), the groove (vibe, key, mood, tempo) or a tip, in turn; the status line shows phase, bar, bars to the drop and five cells of heat. The engine publishes the cycle in `publish_cycle`, and what it turns itself in `Shared::acid_now` (the auto mode's acid sweep) and `filter_now` (the smoothed filter, a breakdown's sweep) for the pads. By hand (`State::Bar`) the header shows the phrase (8 cells), `phrase` says when B lands a drop on the next phrase, and crossings of the energy into the break or past 85 get a line.
- **Auto effects** – `auto_fx(fx, live)` lights an effect button while Solarum uses it by itself: the gate during every build-up (`Live::build`), flickering with the chop.
- **Projects** – a project (`Session`) is the song, the twelve favorites with their names and its file (`project`). Solarum starts with a new, empty project (`new_project`: no groove, no favorites); what was there last time (saved with the window state, also the file's path) waits behind Continue on the start screen (`last_session`), and going back to the start screen keeps the current project there. `saved` remembers tracks, favorites and names as last saved or opened; `dirty` compares, the top bar shows the name with a dot, and New, Open, a recent project or Solari ask first (`pending`, `unsaved_window`: save, don't save, cancel).
- **Favorites 1–12** – `slots: [Option<Song>; 12]`, `names` and `slot_now`, saved with the window state (older states with four slots are padded). `slot_now` only stays while the engine's song is the same groove (`same_groove`); an auto-mode change makes it go dark. A user edit also writes the song into the lit slot; a shuffle, undo or redo detaches it (`slot_now = None`) so a saved pattern is never overwritten by surprise. A click while playing sends `Cmd::Queue` and marks `queued_slot` (it blinks) until the engine reports `Change::Scene`; stopped, it switches at once. **Song** – `song_form` and `song_fallback`; every slot change sends `Cmd::Arrange` again (same form = patterns only), `Change::Part` tells the UI which pattern plays. Song and auto mode exclude each other; stopping ends the song.
- **Settings** – besides volume, output, MIDI, Radar and the update check: `on_start` (`OnStart`: wait, play, or play in the auto mode – sent in `new`), `keep_tempo` (shuffle keeps the BPM), `show_log` (without it the energy stays in the right panel) and `Log::facts`; all saved with the window state.
- **Views** – Planets and Classic; Radar (`View::Orbit`) only when `radar` is on in the settings (saved with the window state).
- **Record** – the top-bar button and R record the session (`capture`, `Cmd::Capture`); there is no pattern recording in the UI any more (`record` stays false; a recorded hit through `Engine::record` would also lift the energy to its track's layer).
- **Mood** – `song::MOODS`, nine of them (scale + progression), in a dropdown with **Auto** on top (names only, the blurb on hover), in the tempo box's menu (`bpm_box`, `tempo_menu`: the tempo, the metronome, and the moods as tiles – Neutral, Auto, then the nine – each with its progression's four chords as steps). `song::NEUTRAL` (plain minor, no changes) is where Solarum starts and a clear goes back to; switching the auto mode on turns Neutral into a mood of the vibe (`start_growing`). The generator always picks one of the vibe's moods (`gen::moods_for`), and a new chapter may switch to another of them; a mood picked by hand sets `Song::mood_hold`, so shuffle and new chapters keep it. A new mood picked with the auto mode on refits the bass and synth notes to it (`gen::refit_notes`) and unmutes the synth, which carries it; by hand it only snaps the notes into the new scale (`gen::snap_notes`) and leaves the mutes alone. What the auto mode does is there by hand too, written into the pattern: right-click on a track's name (`track_menu`: `gen::new_notes`, `gen::add_ghosts` / `remove_ghosts` – `Step::ghost` plays at half velocity and is drawn smaller/fainter – and `gen::set_chance` for thinning), on DROP a fill (`Cmd::Fill`: this bar's second half, or the next bar's once it's under way; into a waiting pattern always now), on a favorite *Switch with a clap roll*. Likewise a synth sound of another family rolls a fitting line only in the auto mode (`set_sound`). Swing comes from the vibe. **Custom** (`Vibe::Custom`) clears every unlocked track; shuffle and the auto mode then follow hypnotic rules (`Vibe::rules`).
- **Orbit** – `Geometry::shown` can still draw a loop shorter than 16 repeated around its ring, but songs no longer have one.
- **Your own sounds** – the project holds up to `SAMPLE_SLOTS` (32) slots (`SolarumApp::sample_slots`, library ids, saved with the session and in the file); `sync_samples` renders each one (`Library::sound`) every frame and sends `Cmd::Samples` when an `Arc` changed, so edits are heard at once. The engine never frees: replaced buffers wait in `retired` until only the UI holds them. A track whose sound is `CUSTOM + k` plays slot k's `SampleBuf` in `trigger` instead of its synth (the whole sound, cut by a decay below the middle, at `sampler::LEVEL` of the track; bass and synth: pitched by the note and the key – a sound counts as C – and ducked under the kick like their own sounds). New recordings and single imports are drafts (`Entry::draft`): out of the list and the sound menus until Save, removed at the next start; a slot that empties or whose sound moves to another track sets the tracks on it back to sound 0. The library's audition (`Cmd::Audition`) is mixed in after the master. Audio files dropped onto the window go into the library.
- **Sounds** – `Track::sound` indexes `Voice::sounds()` (names for space: Pulsar, Comet, Nebula …; `synth.rs` and `gen.rs` keep their old makings in the comments) (or a sample slot, see above). Changing the synth to another family (`gen::synth_family`: 303, stabs (stab, organ), arp (pluck, supersaw), hoover, pad) rolls a line written for the new sound (undo keeps the old one). Changing a sound while stopped plays a preview after the new song went out (`preview`).
- **Update check** – `update::check` runs `curl` against the GitHub releases API on a thread at start (unless switched off in the help window, `check_updates`), compares the tag with `CARGO_PKG_VERSION` and the top bar shows an update button.
- **Solo** – remembers everyone's mute state and puts it back when the solo ends.
- **Devices** – every 3 s the UI checks the number of MIDI ports and the name of the default audio output. New port count → reconnect MIDI; another default output (headphones) or a failed stream (`audio_failed`, set by cpal's error callback) → `restart_audio` builds a new engine on new channels and reconnects MIDI to it.
- **Loaded state is repaired** with `Song::sanitize` (lengths 1–16, finite volumes, tempo 90–180 …), also when the engine receives a song.
- **Shared files are untrusted** – `SolarumFile::load` refuses files over 160 MB, `Library::unpack` takes at most `SAMPLE_SLOTS` sounds of at most `MAX_SECONDS` each and repairs their edits (`Edit::sanitize`, also for `library.ron`); a rendered sound never holds NaN. If the output still goes non-finite, the master plays silence and sets `Shared::broken`, and the UI's 3-second device check builds a fresh engine.
- **Saving** goes through `file::write_atomic` (a `.tmp` beside the file, synced, renamed over it): projects, recordings, the library index and its WAVs.
- **The microphone** (`Recorder`) fills a `Take` allocated for 30 s up front, with a peak per 256 samples; the screen draws only those peaks (`snapshot`), so the input callback neither allocates nor waits long for the lock.

## Generator

`gen::generate(vibe, macros, rng)` first picks a sound per track that suits the vibe (`sound_for`), then builds every track from genre rules: kick on 0/4/8/12 plus ghost kicks that always play (electro: broken patterns), claps on 2 and 4, hats as 16ths, off-beats or shakers, Euclidean rims and rides that stay off kick and clap, a bass template that never sits on a kick. The synth line depends on its sound: a 303 line that lands on a chord note on every beat and wanders in between, off-beat chord stabs (also the organ), a plucked arpeggio (also the supersaw), a hoover riff or pad chords that the engine holds until the next one. Bass and every synth line follow a two-chord progression derived from key and scale (`progression`) – the bass always plays the chord's root (or its fifth) – so they agree even when only one track is rolled again. Shorter loops are 8 or 12 steps and only on percussion. Tests check the rules for every vibe (`every_vibe_makes_a_techno_groove`, `the_tracks_fit_together`).

`gen::evolve` changes exactly one thing. A big change (a phrase start) alternates between the drums (clap, rim, hats, ride) and bass/synth: new notes on the same rhythm, a new pattern, another sound from the vibe's set (`sounds_for`), or a track in or out (at least four stay; a muted synth comes back most readily). A small touch shifts a rim, ride or 303 line or loops a half bar of rim, ride or open hat – the clap never leaves the backbeat. `gen::Recent` remembers the last two tracks changed; neither is touched next. It never touches the kick and allocates nothing (`pick_if`, `ALL_STEPS`), as it runs on the audio thread – `the_auto_mode_renders_without_allocating` counts allocations with a test-only global allocator and fails on any. (Still allocating there: the boxed `Event::Song` and the recording chunks.)

**The director** (`ear.rs`) picks the auto mode's changes by ear (`Engine::auto_change` → `ear::choose`). It runs `gen::evolve` on `CANDIDATES` (6) copies of the song, each with its own `Rng` forked from `auto_rng` (a `Song` is `Copy`, so nothing allocates), plus on a big change one *reprise*: the unlocked track furthest from the cycle's theme (`Engine::home`, the tracks as `new_cycle` or the drop left them) set back to it (`Change::Reprise`). `ear::hear` listens to one bar: `density` (hits without the kick, ghosts and chance steps weighted), `clash` (share of bass notes on a kick) and `crowd` (steps with four or more tracks). The score: −clash, −crowd, the density's distance from `density_goal(energy)`, the step size (`gen::distance` to now against 1.5 for a big change, 0.6 for a touch; nothing audible is heavily penalized), the drift from the theme (`ear::drift`, mutes ignored) against `drift_goal(progress, heat)` – rising to two thirds of the groove part of the cycle, then falling back before the drop – the `Taste` bonus and a little luck. The winner's `Why` (room for the kick, more air, more drive, the theme returns) rides along in `Event::Song`'s fourth field when it was the reason; the log appends it. Tracks in `Engine::hands_off` (edited by hand – one or two tracks whose steps, length or sound changed in a `Cmd::Song` while the auto mode runs – for 16 bars, their edit also becomes the theme) count as locked for the candidates and for `new_chapter`, and get their real lock flags back afterwards. `Taste` (per track and per kind, `Change::kind`, ±6) comes from the thumbs in SOLAR OS (`Cmd::Taste`; the UI keeps it in the settings and puts the track back for a 👎 from `SolarumApp::last_auto`). `Cmd::Drive` (−1 … 1, from SOLAR OS's energy slider while AUTO FX steers, or `turnup` / `chill` in thirds) lifts `engine::curve` by `0.25 × drive`, adds `0.3 × drive` to the heat (`heat_now`: the plan, the cycle length, the director's drift) and pushes `ear::density_goal` by up to ±8 hits; the energy goal moves at once (`Shared::drive` lets the map draw the lifted curve). The UI glides the tempo `3 × Δdrive` BPM (`glide_bpm`); with AUTO FX off the slider sets the energy and the commands glide it (`glide_energy`) – a hand on either ends its glide. The engine also publishes `Shared::cycle_depth` and `next_change` so SOLAR OS can draw the cycle's map with `engine::curve`, the same function `curve_target` steers by.

## UI

Immediate mode: `SolarumApp::ui` draws everything every frame (continuously while playing). The orbit and pads are custom-painted with the egui painter (circles, arcs, a mesh for the beam). `Geometry` in `orbit.rs` maps steps to positions and back for hit testing.

Layout: top bar (the vibe dropdown and a small shuffle button; on the right the PLANETS | CLASSIC (| RADAR) switch (`widgets::segmented`), the visualizer menu (`controls::visualizer_card`: LIGHTS and FACE as pictures) and the red record button with the AUTO SONG arrow (`widgets::rec_split`)), tracks on the left (row height adapts to the window; the sound pill opens the sounds (built-in, then yours)), the sound (the character pad, the space/pump/pitch/drive cards (2 × 2), the EQ curve – `controls.rs`) that the SOUND heading folds away (`sound_open`, saved, folded at first) / LIVE: energy (unless it's in the log), the filter pad, effects (2 × 3), drop on the right, the orbit or pads in the middle with the pattern strip below (and the song strip above it while a song plays). No status bar: the shortcuts are in the help (?), volume, sound output, MIDI and the update check in the settings (gear), "No sound" shows in the top bar, and nothing else at the bottom of the right panel; the LIVE heading folds energy, filter, effects and DROP away (`live_open`, `live_controls`). Right-click on a step (orbit or grid) opens its menu (`orbit::step_context_menu`). Colour fills use opaque `mix(PANEL, colour, k)` – translucent colours read washed out on the concrete. The right panel keeps one `GAP` (8 px) between all its controls, across and down, and every card, pad and effect button is dark inside (`theme::PAD`). Look: concrete around everything (`widgets::concrete` on the background layer: soft clouds, mottling, grain and air pores, drawn with exact premultiplied shades – `alpha()` blends white in linear light, far too bright for a texture this subtle; the panels are transparent on it, without separator lines) and a window into deep space in the middle (`theme::SPACE` and a parallax `widgets::star_field`, set into the concrete with `widgets::bezel_frame` like SOLAR OS whose drift is integrated per frame so speed changes never jump); the visualizer uses the same space and stars. The character pad gets whatever height is left; short windows and an open sound get smaller effect buttons and DROP. Minimum window size is 1100 × 700.

The UI fonts have no ♯, ◂ or ▸ – use `#` and words instead.

## Checking changes

There is no way to drive the real window from a terminal. Instead:

- `cargo test --release render_ -- --ignored` writes screenshots to `target/snapshots/*.png` (orbit stopped/playing/build-up, a song with a waiting pattern + update button, pads, small window with the knobs open and locks, help, visualizer, and the face at rest, in a build-up, on the drop, bursting and coming back (`render_face_frames`), and `readme` – the source of `docs/images/solarum.jpg`, the README's only picture: `sips -s format jpeg -s formatOptions 82 -Z 2000`) and demo loops for every vibe plus a 68-bar auto-mode run to `target/snapshots/sounds/*.wav`.
- `cargo test --release print_levels -- --ignored --nocapture` prints the loudness of each track and each of its sounds alone – for balancing the mix (sounds of one track should stay within ~3 dB of each other).
